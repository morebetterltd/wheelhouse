142:**A provider-side failure blocks a seat.** Credit balance, quota, 429, expired-token, invalid-token, and similar provider/auth failures are often transient or operator-fixable faster than a handoff round. Do not park the bead on the principal after the first failure. Run `bun seats/adapter.ts probe <seat>` at about two, five, and ten minutes after the failure, and run it again on every commander tick while the bead remains blocked. The probe is a one-line liveness turn outside any bead worktree, so it records no bead comment and does not spend the seat's work session. If any probe answers `OK`, close the blocked bead as commander-owned recovery and resume the loop. Only after those probes keep failing do you say "waiting on the principal", and the record should include the latest probe error rather than a summary guess.
