# 1.1.0 — dispatcher-owned drive evidence

A new rule makes the retention location explicit: evidence of every attempted
mapped drive stays under the dispatcher's named `--out`, outside product source.
The ending FINDINGS protocol is unchanged. Historical 1.0.0 runs keep their
version; do not restamp them. A new pass reads the current skill, drives the map
again and records the version actually used with its retained evidence.
