## Signal
Project: Basket Ledger, an invented shared grocery notebook.
Kind: isa-gap
Title: People cannot tell when their grocery list is out of date
Open claim GL7: People are told when their shared grocery list may be out of date because the connection is lost.
Probe: Open a shared grocery list, disconnect from the network, and return to the list. The old list still looks current, with no explanation that changes from other people may be missing. GL7 remains open.

## Intent
Intent:
Make it clear when a shared grocery list may be out of date because the connection is lost. People using Basket Ledger need to distinguish an old view from current information so they can make shopping decisions without being misled about what others have changed.
